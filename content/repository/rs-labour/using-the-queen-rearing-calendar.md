Queen rearing runs on a fixed biological clock: graft day, cell sealing, emergence, mating flights, laying confirmation. None of those intervals negotiate. The attached **queen rearing calendar** spreadsheet turns that clock into a season plan.

## How to use it

Enter a target date — when you need mated queens ready — and work backwards. The sheet lays out when the cell builder needs to be queenless, when to graft, when cells must move to the incubator or mating nucs, when virgins emerge, and the earliest realistic date to check for laying. Batch entries let you stagger multiple rounds through a season without the rounds colliding on equipment.

## Why this matters at volume

One round of queens fails politely; a season of overlapping rounds fails expensively. The calendar's real value is showing conflicts *before* they happen — two batches wanting the same mating nucs in the same week is visible in the sheet in July, invisible in the field until December.
