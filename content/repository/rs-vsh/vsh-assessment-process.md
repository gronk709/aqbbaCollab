Most published VSH protocols assume a research station: liquid nitrogen on tap, a laboratory bench, and someone paid to count cells. The attached **VSH process for small scale** document is the working answer to a more useful question — what can a member with a dozen colonies and a weekend actually run?

## What it covers

The document steps through the assessment cycle as practised in the program's collective assessments: selecting and marking test colonies, the infestation measurement baseline, running the hygienic response test, the count windows, and how to record results so they're comparable with everyone else's.

## Why comparability is the whole game

A VSH score means nothing in isolation — it only becomes selection-grade information when your 78% and another member's 78% were measured the same way. That's why this document exists, and why the program's collective assessment days run to it. If you assess at home, run the same process, record the same fields, and your data joins the pool; improvise, and it can't.
