The documents attached to this sub-topic cover the hands-on side of queen rearing: methods, procedures, schedules and cell-building processes.

## What's here and how it fits together

The **COLOSS standard methods paper** (Büchler, Andonov, Bienefeld et al.) is the scientific reference standard — a comprehensive treatment of rearing methods, technical equipment, quality control, and the statistics behind estimating breeding values. When members disagree about technique, this is the arbiter we point to.

The **Odd Acres queen rearing procedures** are one member apiary's practical interpretation of those methods — a working protocol you can follow step by step, developed and refined over multiple seasons. The **colony preparation notes** cover getting a cell-raising colony into the right state before any grafting happens, which is where most first attempts actually fail.

The **cell run process diagram** is a one-page visual of the stages of a cell run from graft to emergence. Print it and put it on the shed wall.

The **Miller method steps** spreadsheet covers the graft-free alternative: if your eyesight or hand-steadiness makes grafting frustrating, the Miller method gets you queen cells without touching a larva.

## Suggested reading order

1. Colony preparation notes — the right colony state comes first
2. Odd Acres procedures — the working protocol, end to end
3. Cell run process diagram — keep it beside you during the run
4. The COLOSS standard methods paper — the deep reference, as questions come up
